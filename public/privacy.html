Privacy policy

Marygold Collections (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Information we collect

  • Order details — name, email, phone number, delivery address so we can fulfil your order.
  • Account activity — cart contents are stored locally on your device; we never see your password (there are no customer accounts).
  • Communications — if you contact us, we keep the message so we can help you.

How we use it

We use your information only to process orders, arrange delivery, provide support, and — with your consent — send occasional offers and updates via our newsletter.

What we don’t do

  • We never sell or rent your personal information to third parties.
  • We never store payment card details.
  • We never store passwords in plain text.

Data retention & security

Order records are kept to honour warranties and returns. Access to the administration area is protected by server-side authentication and secure session cookies.

Your rights

You can request a copy or deletion of your data at any time by contacting onweebi12@gmail.com.
